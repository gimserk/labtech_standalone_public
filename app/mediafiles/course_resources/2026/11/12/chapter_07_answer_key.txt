Chapter 7 Answer Key
1. c) Vishing (Vishing is phishing conducted over a voice call.)
2. d) The sender's email address domain does not match the company's official
domain. (While the others are red flags, this is the hardest for a scammer to fake  and the easiest to verify.)
3. c) Pretexting (Pretexting involves creating a detailed backstory or pretext to build
trust.)
4. d) Close the email and log into your bank's website using a trusted bookmark or
by typing the address yourself. (This uses an independent, trusted channel for  verification.)
5. d) Greed (The scam is appealing to the victim's desire for a prize.)
6. a) Smishing (Smishing is phishing conducted via SMS text message.)
7. c) Hover your mouse over the link to see its true destination URL. (This reveals if
the link text is masking a malicious URL.)
8. b) The IRS does not make threatening phone calls and never demands payment
in gift cards. (This method of payment is a classic sign of a scam.)
9. d) Your cell phone carrier (7726, which spells SPAM, is the universal number for
reporting smishing to carriers.)
10. a) Social Engineering (This is the umbrella term for all attacks that target human
psychology.)
