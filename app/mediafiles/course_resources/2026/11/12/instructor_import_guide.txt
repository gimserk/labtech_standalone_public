IMPORT
1. In LabTech, open Import Course as a staff user.
2. Upload this .coursepkg file (not the original PDF).
3. LabTech creates an INACTIVE copy with prefix "Import of ".
4. Open the course content manager to inspect pages, resources, quizzes and labs.
5. Activate the course after reviewing and confirming visibility.

IMPORTANT CONTENT & RELEASE NOTES
- All contents-section entries are converted to TextPage EXCEPT the twelve answer keys.
- Answer keys are stored in Course Resources with visible_to_students=False.
- The original PDF is stored as a student-visible textbook resource.
- Text pages contain source prose and all embedded original figures, where present.
- The Chapter 6 quiz is mislabeled "Chapter 5 Quiz" in the source PDF; this import
  identifies the quiz in Chapter 6 correctly without rewriting the source book.
- Quiz questions are copied from the text with correct multiple-choice selections.
- Native quizzes are initially UNPUBLISHED so instructors can review exact-match
  short-answer grading rules (and accept additional valid answers).
- 12 native Lab objects are created as UNPUBLISHED guided lab steps with NO VM templates.
  They are based on the textbook's physical/self-directed lab instructions. If your
  LabTech deployment expects machines, assign templates and test prior to publishing.
- Each lab and critical-thinking exercise also has a separate graded file-submission
  activity for documenting student work.
- Nothing is automatically assigned to real Proxmox templates or student accounts.
- The current LabTech validator rejects top-level rubrics and learning_outcomes
  despite the exporter sometimes writing them, so those sections are not present
  in this import package. The actual textbook is reproduced in course content.
- Figures were included from the user's PDF; there are no external dependencies.
